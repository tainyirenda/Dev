Why These Markets?

High Informal Trade

These markets have significant informal economies with active street vendors, spaza shops, and dukas that can benefit from our platform.

Mobile Penetration

Strong mobile phone adoption and WhatsApp usage, making our mobile-first approach highly accessible to local traders.

Strategic Locations

Geographic diversity across East, West, and Southern Africa, enabling us to understand different market dynamics.
